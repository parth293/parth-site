## How I'm screening these

Same two filters as the protein tracker — thesis-heavy over coursework-heavy,
and an active research group with a track record of taking its own thesis
students into PhD slots — plus one addition specific to this list, from
[what I found looking at the field
broadly](/writing/bioprocess-engineering-in-pharma): I'm weighting labs
working on viral vector purification, mRNA-LNP mixing/scale-up, or
scale-down modeling for continuous downstream processing higher than generic
"biopharmaceutical manufacturing" labs, since those are the areas where the
open problems actually are right now, not mAb platform work.

Professor and student names are only filled in once verified against a lab
page or publication, same as the other tracker.
