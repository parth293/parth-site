This is the working list behind [the reframe from whey prices to protein
production cost in India](/writing/protein-production-cost-in-india). It's a
tracker, not an essay — I'm adding to it every time I look into a new
program, so treat anything below as the state of my research on the date
this was last updated, not a finished conclusion.

This tracker is scoped to **food and dairy protein** — whey, alternative
protein, precision fermentation for nutrition. Programs and labs aimed at
**pharmaceutical bioprocessing** — viral vectors, mRNA-LNP, biosimilars,
mAb downstream processing — are split out into their own [pharma bioprocess
tracker](/writing/grad-school-tracker-pharma-bioprocess), since a lot of
university names show up on both lists but the actual lab you'd target
inside them is different.

## How I'm screening these

Two filters, before a university's general ranking matters at all:

1. **Thesis-heavy, not coursework-heavy.** I want 5–7 months of near
   full-time lab work inside an active group, not a token final-semester
   project. That's the only version of a master's that functions as a trial
   PhD.
2. **An active research group already working on bioprocessing, precision
   fermentation, or protein purification** — ideally one where the professor
   has a track record of taking their own strong thesis students into PhD
   positions, since that's the friction a cold PhD application doesn't have
   to clear.

Professor and student names below are only filled in once I've actually
verified them against a lab page or publication — "not identified yet" means
exactly that, not that no one's there.

## Other funding routes worth keeping in view

Not tied to a specific university yet, so not in the tables above:

- **Commonwealth Scholarships (UK)** — better fit for the food-policy/economics lever than the lab-science one.
- **Erasmus Mundus** — joint master's across multiple EU countries; useful if I want exposure to more than one dairy-processing or bioprocessing tradition before committing to a PhD country.
- **National Overseas Scholarship (Government of India)** — India's own scheme for funding study abroad; "helping reduce a strategic import-dependence problem" is close to the framing these applications reward.
