## AI and differentiation

It's never been easier to learn what works. And it's never been harder to
stand out. Access to the how-to has stopped being the bottleneck; everyone can
get the same answer to "how do I do X" in the same afternoon. What's scarce is
no longer the recipe — it's whatever made you reach for that recipe instead of
a different one.

Taste, judgment, and responsibility become more valuable as AI becomes more
capable. The model will execute almost anything you can specify precisely. The
part that doesn't commoditize is deciding what's worth specifying, catching
when the output is technically correct but wrong for the situation, and being
the one who's accountable when it is wrong.
