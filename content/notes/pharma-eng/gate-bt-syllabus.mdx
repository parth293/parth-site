The GATE 2027 Biotechnology (BT) syllabus, published by the organizing
institute (IIT Madras), broken into three levels: **section** → **topic** →
**item**. Each item is numbered (`section.topic.item`) so it can be used as a
standalone tag when categorizing past-paper questions — tag a question with
its item number, then roll counts up to topic or section to see which regions
score best.

## 1. Engineering Mathematics

### 1.1 Linear Algebra

- 1.1.1 Matrices and determinants
- 1.1.2 Systems of linear equations
- 1.1.3 Eigenvalues and eigenvectors

### 1.2 Calculus

- 1.2.1 Multivariable calculus: limits, continuity, and differentiability
- 1.2.2 Partial derivatives, maxima and minima
- 1.2.3 Sequences and series
- 1.2.4 Test for convergence
- 1.2.5 Multivariable calculus: gradient, divergence, and curl (applications in fluid mechanics, transport processes)

### 1.3 Differential Equations

- 1.3.1 Linear and nonlinear first-order ODEs
- 1.3.2 Higher-order ODEs with constant coefficients
- 1.3.3 Cauchy's and Euler's equations
- 1.3.4 Laplace transforms

### 1.4 Probability and Statistics

- 1.4.1 Mean, median, mode, and standard deviation
- 1.4.2 Random variables
- 1.4.3 Poisson, normal, and binomial distributions
- 1.4.4 Correlation and regression analysis
- 1.4.5 Bayesian statistics

### 1.5 Numerical Methods

- 1.5.1 Solution of linear and nonlinear algebraic equations
- 1.5.2 Integration by trapezoidal and Simpson's rule
- 1.5.3 Single-step methods for differential equations

- 2.1.1 Biomolecules — structure and function
