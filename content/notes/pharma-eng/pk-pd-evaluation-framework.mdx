This is the hub for a set of private notes evaluating whether a research
masters/PhD in some corner of pharmacokinetics and pharmacodynamics is
worth pursuing, and if so, which corner. This page and the ones it links
to are gated — draft notes, only visible with the unlock link, kept out of
the public build entirely. This is a first pass, generated with AI
assistance against my own profile, and it needs a real second pass —
verifying named professors, actual funding numbers, and actual salary
data — before any of it should influence a real decision. I'm keeping it
here specifically so it stays in git, private, but not lost in a Downloads
folder.

## Why this exists

I have a pharmaceutical engineering degree from IIT BHU (closer to chemical
engineering than to pharmacy) and six years at Leucine, a GMP compliance
software company, growing from implementation into a director-level role
spanning product, AI, and enterprise delivery. I'm weighing a
research-oriented masters/PhD against a startup or consulting path in the
same specialisation. This set of notes is me trying to figure out, before
committing years to it, whether a PK/PD PhD is actually a good idea, and
which of its subfields fits my actual background rather than sounding good
in the abstract.

## The rubric

Each subfield got evaluated across six blocks:

1. **Understanding the field** — what it is, where it sits in the drug
   pipeline, how mature versus unsolved it is.
2. **PhD feasibility** — funding, admission odds with my specific profile,
   typical duration, required skills, advisor availability, and whether my
   background actually transfers.
3. **AI and automation risk** — is this being automated, or does AI mostly
   amplify the people already in it, and what's the 5–10 year outlook.
4. **Industry career after the PhD** — exit quality, whether a postdoc is
   actually needed, target roles, rough salary bands, big pharma versus
   biotech, geography.
5. **Immigration and settlement** — US, Canada, Australia, UK/Germany/
   Switzerland, ranked for my specific situation.
6. **Pre-PhD credibility building** — what I could actually do right now,
   cheaply, to build a real application before committing to anything.

Each subfield note ends with a 1–5 scorecard across ten dimensions pulled
from those six blocks. None of the specific numbers in any of the notes
below are independently verified — they're an AI-assisted first pass, and
I'm treating them exactly the way I treat the grad-school trackers in
[/writing](/writing): the state of my thinking on a date, not settled
research.

## What I actually trust from this pass

The mechanism-level explanations of what each subfield is — now rewritten
and published as the public notes this set links back to — hold up fine;
that's descriptive science, easy to sanity-check, and I did. Everything
with a specific number attached — a funding amount, a named lab still
being active, a salary band, a visa timeline — is unverified and I'd treat
it as a hypothesis to check, not a fact to plan around. The next real step
here isn't writing more of these; it's picking two or three of the
top-ranked subfields and actually verifying the specific advisor and
funding claims, the way the grad-school trackers already do for
bioprocess engineering.
